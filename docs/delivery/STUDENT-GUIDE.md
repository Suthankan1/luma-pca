# LUMA student guide

- **Activate**: open the link in your invitation email and choose a password.
- **My classes**: your classes for the next four weeks, in each class's time zone, and your batches.
- **My attendance**: once a class is final you see present, late, absent or excused and how long you were connected.
  Join Zoom with the email the academy registered for you, so you are recognised.
- **Appeal**: if a result looks wrong, choose "Appeal this", explain, and send. Staff reply with a note.
- **Reactions and questions** (only if the academy uses them): you choose whether to take part, and separately whether
  staff may see your name in rankings. You can change your mind at any time. During a class, open it to react or answer.
- **Sign out** when you finish on a shared device.
